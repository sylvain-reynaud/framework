# Persona Quality

| Check | Pass when |
| --- | --- |
| Evidence-backed | every consequential claim has a status and a basis or next check |
| Decision-changing | at least one product or experience decision would differ because this persona exists |
| Distinct | this persona differs from every sibling persona on at least one axis: job, motivation, or context |
| First screen | what they want to see first is named and ranked |
| Return trigger | the rhythm names what brings them back |
| Anti-persona | who this persona is not is named, not left implicit |
