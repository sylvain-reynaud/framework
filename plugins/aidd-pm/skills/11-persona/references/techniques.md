# Discovery Techniques

Choose a lens only when it resolves a live uncertainty.

| Uncertainty | Lens | Useful result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Motivation or progress | Jobs To Be Done | situation, desired progress, and current alternative |
| Meaningful group differences | Behavioral segmentation | differences that would change a product decision |
| What drives engagement | Hexad motivation types | which of philanthropist, socializer, free spirit, achiever, player, or disruptor motivates them, and what to build for it |
| Vague or assumed behavior | Journey sampling | one real recent session, step by step, with friction and feelings named |
| Persona overlap or scope creep | Anti-persona | who this product deliberately does not serve |
| What they open the app to answer | First screen question | the one question they come to answer, ranked above the fold |
