# Emotional Arc

Entry, during, and exit for a screen or a journey, applied per persona.

| Phase | Question to answer |
| --- | --- |
| Entry | what state is the persona in right before this, and what do they need to feel first |
| During | what keeps them oriented and confident while they act |
| Exit | what they feel leaving, and what they carry to the next screen or session |

| Rule | Check |
| --- | --- |
| Peak-end rule | the arc is judged by its most intense moment and its ending, not its average |
| First use vs return | a first-use arc teaches; a return arc gets out of the way |
| Reassurance before information | when stakes are high (money, health, an irreversible action), reassurance precedes information |
