# Metrics

Metrics that justify a mechanic, plus the anxiety guardrails that justify pulling one back. Each names the decision it can change.

| Metric | Kind | What it measures | Decision it can change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Activation | justify | share of new users who complete the core action once | whether the first-use trigger and reward are clear enough |
| Return rate | justify | share of users back within the habit rhythm window | whether the return trigger or reward is working |
| Core-action frequency | justify | how often the core action repeats per active user | whether the loop's reward is strong enough to sustain repetition |
| Streak health (recoveries vs breaks) | guardrail | ratio of freezes or recoveries used to streaks broken outright | whether the recovery rule is too strict or too generous |
| Opt-out rate | guardrail | share of users disabling a mechanic | whether that mechanic is unwanted or miscalibrated; a rising rate means redesign or remove it |
| Complaints mentioning pressure or guilt | guardrail | count of support or review mentions of anxiety, pressure, guilt | whether a mechanic crossed into a dark pattern; triggers a safeguard review |
| Churn after a broken streak or missed goal | guardrail | retention delta for users who just missed a mechanic's expectation | whether the mechanic punishes a lapse hard enough to drive users away |
