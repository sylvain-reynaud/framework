# Ranking

## Premium gap

A gap a persona feels on a frequent path that a competitor-grade product would not have.

## Severity scale

| Severity | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Blocking | stops the persona from completing their job |
| Felt | the job completes, but the persona notices friction or a missing polish |
| Polish | cosmetic or minor; a competitor-grade product would still fix it |

## Score

| Factor | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Persona impact | how much the gap hurts the persona's job: blocking > felt > polish |
| Frequency | how often the persona hits this path |
| Score | persona impact multiplied by frequency |
| Tie-break | when two gaps score equal, the lower-effort fix ranks first |

## Shortlist rule

Keep the top 5 scored gaps as premium gaps. Move every other scored finding to the backlog, ordered by score.
